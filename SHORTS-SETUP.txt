NIGHTFLIX SHORTS SETUP

1) Upload shorts.html and shorts.json beside index.html and movies.json.
2) Add one object per licensed short clip to shorts.json, for example:

[
  {
    "movieId": 182,
    "title": "Scene (Telugu)",
    "video": "https://your-cdn.example/scene-short.mp4",
    "caption": "Watch the full title on NIGHTFLIX",
    "poster": "https://your-cdn.example/poster.jpg"
  }
]

movieId must exactly match the id of the related entry in movies.json.
video must be a direct, browser-playable video URL (prefer MP4/H.264). Use only clips you own or are licensed to publish.

GESTURES
- Swipe DOWN: next short (as requested).
- Swipe UP: previous short.
- Swipe LEFT: open the related title at movie.html?id=MOVIE_ID.
- Tap Movie button: open related title.
- Sound button: toggle audio.

Note: The existing project uses movie.html?id=... for movie detail pages (not movies.html).
